# Bloodborne Pathogen Exposure Control Plan

## Scope
This plan applies to all nursing staff of the (fictional) Example Health Department.

## Needlestick and sharps injuries
Wash the site with soap and water. Report the exposure to the nursing supervisor within one hour
and complete the incident report the same day. Post-exposure evaluation must start within two hours.

Example of a completed form field (do not copy): SSN 900-12-3456.

## Hand hygiene
Use alcohol-based hand rub before and after every client contact.
