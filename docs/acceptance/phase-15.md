# Acceptance script: Phase 15 (chronic disease prevention and community assessment)

Run these prompts in Claude Desktop with the synthetic corpus (`fixtures/synthetic/data`) in the mounted folder and the `chronic`, `equity`, `epi`, `env`, and `core` toolsets enabled (for example `PHN_TOOLSETS=chronic,equity,epi,env,core`). Record pass/fail and notes.

Rows 1 to 11 cover `community_bp_screening_toolkit` and `diabetes_prevention_finder`; 12 to 22 cover `preventive_services_lookup` and `asthma_home_assessment`; 23 to 34 cover the community health assessment tools and prompt; 35 to 41 cover `geocode_and_aggregate_addresses`.
