# Acceptance script: Phase 16 (program planning, evaluation, and evidence)

Run these prompts in Claude Desktop with the synthetic corpus (`fixtures/synthetic/data`) in the mounted folder and the `planning`, `ebp`, `epi`, and `core` toolsets enabled (for example `PHN_TOOLSETS=planning,ebp,epi,core`). Record pass/fail and notes.

Rows 1 to 11 cover `build_logic_model`, `write_smartie_objectives`, and `build_evaluation_plan`; 12 to 24 cover `find_evidence_based_interventions`, `apply_behavior_change_theory`, and `track_program_performance`; 25 to 34 cover `appraise_evidence` and `build_evidence_synthesis_table`; 35 to 45 cover `plan_quality_improvement`.
