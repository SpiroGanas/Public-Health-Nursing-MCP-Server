# Fragment: Phase 18 workforce (LEAD-06, LEAD-07, LEAD-08)

The coordinator folds these sections into `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/acceptance/phase-18.md`, and
`STATUS.md`. Package key `workforce`; branch `phase-18-workforce`. Registered tools 135 to 138;
`TOOL_COUNT` in `docs/user-guide/guide-content.js` is 138 in this branch.

## Changelog

- Leadership tools (toolset `leadership`):
  - `assess_workforce_competencies` (LEAD-06): self-assessments against the eight domains and three
    tiers of the Council on Linkages Core Competencies for Public Health Professionals (2021) or
    the Quad Council Community/Public Health Nursing Competencies (2018), on the four-level scale
    (none, aware, knowledgeable, proficient). With no files it writes a blank workbook (XLSX with
    live formulas: ratings form, team results that hide any result for fewer than 3 people, a
    training plan sheet, a credential tracker, and DPH's standards) and a Word guide. With a ratings
    file (staff code, tier, domain, rating) it returns team means by domain, gaps to a target,
    means by tier, a ranked training plan, and writes the workbook and a DOCX plan. With a
    credentials file (staff code, position, hire date, credentials completed) it tracks the
    Massachusetts local public health workforce standards from the bundled `ma-performance-standards`
    snapshot (for example MAVEN training within 6 months and the Foundations course within 1 year
    for a public health nurse): status by requirement and position, with the same small-group rule.
    Staff are identified by code; name, phone, and email columns are refused; no code or name is
    ever returned. Competency statements are not bundled (the frameworks' sites were blocked).
  - `board_of_health_meeting_assistant` (LEAD-07): `notice_check` computes the latest time to post
    a meeting notice under the Open Meeting Law (G.L. c. 30A s. 20(b), 940 CMR 29.03): 48 hours,
    leaving out whole Saturdays, Sundays, and Massachusetts legal holidays (G.L. c. 4 s. 7 cl.
    Eighteenth; bundled list for 2026-2028; Suffolk county bodies also skip Evacuation Day and
    Bunker Hill Day), checks a posting time, notice contents, topic specificity, remote access, and
    executive session items; `agenda` writes the agenda and notice draft (DOCX) with staff pages (post-by time, notice
    checklist, chair's responsibilities, executive session script); `staff_report` writes a
    report template; `minutes` drafts minutes (DOCX) from the nurse's notes file or text, checks the
    parts s. 22(a) and 940 CMR 29.10-29.11 require, scans the notes for identifiers (line numbers
    only), and never returns the notes; `oml_guide` returns the requirements with sources (ten
    executive session purposes, minutes contents and approval timing). New content files
    `open_meeting_law.yaml` and `ma_legal_holidays.yaml` (generated by
    `xtask/scripts/build_workforce_holidays.py`, which has `--check`).
  - `draft_standing_orders` (LEAD-08): drafts a standing order or protocol skeleton (vaccine,
    medication, or activity) as a Word file for the medical director's review and signature,
    following Board of Registration in Nursing Advisory Ruling 9324 (ten required elements and
    three limits) and, for vaccines, Advisory Ruling 9804. The nurse's text goes in with the
    source she cites for each clinical section (required); everything else is a `[NURSE: ...]` or
    `[MEDICAL DIRECTOR: ...]` placeholder with signature lines and review dates. No clinical
    content is bundled. Returns section status, element coverage, wording cues that may conflict
    with the limits, and for vaccines DPH's signed 2026-2027 standing orders for local boards of
    health to check first.

## Content review

- `content/leadership/workforce_competencies.yaml` (pending, review_due 2027-02-01). The nurse
  checks: the eight domain names of each framework against the framework documents (2021 names read
  in one open-access 2025 article; 2018 nursing names read in a 2025 nursing article, which words
  the first domain "Assessment Analytic Skills" while another article says "Analytic and
  Assessment Skills": medium confidence); the tier descriptions (medium); the four-level scale and
  the meanings, which are short paraphrases of a scale described in a 2024 article that used a
  Core Competencies assessment (medium; the Public Health Foundation's own instrument wording was
  not read); the rough pairing of 2021 and 2018 domains (low confidence for Health Equity to
  Cultural Competency and Community Partnership to Community Dimensions of Practice); the
  position aliases that match a staff title to a DPH position; the priority cut-offs (gap 1, 0.5),
  the default target of 3, the small-group size of 3, and the generic training steps (this
  project's, no source). NOT bundled because they could not be read: the numbered competency
  statements, the frameworks' reuse terms, PHF's assessment instruments.
- `content/leadership/open_meeting_law.yaml` (pending). High confidence as paraphrases of the
  statute text and the Attorney General's November 2024 checklists read on 2026-11-12. The nurse
  or town counsel checks: the reading that whole Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays are removed from
  the 48-hour count (medium; no Attorney General worked example found); the Attorney General's
  statement that cancellations do not need 48 hours (from a training primer); the remote
  participation note (Chapter 20 of the Acts of 2021 expires June 30, 2027).
- `content/leadership/ma_legal_holidays.yaml` (pending, generated). 2026 and 2027 statewide dates
  agree with the Trial Court's published closing dates (high). 2028 and the two Suffolk-only days are
  computed only (medium). The statute moves a holiday on a Sunday to Monday and not one on a
  Saturday; whether a Suffolk board of health counts Evacuation Day and Bunker Hill Day is for town
  counsel (the statute says Suffolk offices stay open on them).
- `content/leadership/standing_orders.yaml` (pending). Advisory Rulings 9324 and 9804 paraphrased
  (high). The vaccine order outline comes from one Immunize.org template and one DPH template
  (medium). The skeleton's section list and placeholder wording are this project's design. The
  Immunize.org permission statement was read in one template only. No clinical content is bundled.
- Credential rules: not a content file; they come from the existing `ma-performance-standards`
  snapshot (DPH's workbook modified 2024-07-15). The deadline parsing reads "within N months or
  years of hire" from DPH's wording; waivers and equivalents are not modeled.

## Open items

- **Blocked sources.** phf.org, archive.phf.org, quadcouncilphn.org, nursingworld.org, naccho.org,
  astho.org, www.sec.state.ma.us (the Secretary's holiday pages), www.umassd.edu, www.doe.mass.edu,
  openstax.org, and pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov article pages (E-utilities `efetch` for PMC works) were
  refused. malegislature.gov, mass.gov (plain curl), immunize.org, and cdc.gov (www) worked. mass.gov
  `/doc/<name>/download` returned Word files for the Board's advisory rulings even with a `.pdf`
  look; check the file type before running `pdftotext`.
- **Competency statements** of both frameworks are the main gap. When a person can read the
  Council on Linkages PDF and the Quad Council document, add the statement IDs and short
  paraphrases (check their reuse terms) and extend the ratings file with a competency column.
- **Credential matching** uses keywords (acronyms such as MAVEN and RS, first significant word
  otherwise); unmatched entries are counted, not named. A controlled list in the workbook's
  Credentials sheet would be better.
- **The 48-hour count** is this project's reading; confirm with the Attorney General's Division
  of Open Government and the clerk. The calculator covers meeting dates 2026-01-06 to 2028-12-31;
  extend `ma_legal_holidays.yaml` (rerun the generator, update `FIRST_DAY`/`LAST_DAY` in
  `crates/phn-tools/src/leadership/oml.rs`) each year.
- **Notes parsing** for minutes is label based (Present:, Item:, Motion:, Vote:, Exhibit:,
  Executive session:); it does not summarize discussion. The nurse edits the draft.
- **Not modeled:** remote participation eligibility, the clerk's filing time, hearing notice under
  other statutes, executive session minute release review dates, the Open Meeting Law complaint
  process, staff report data tables, standing order clinical checks, the authentication time frame.
- **Formula results.** Cells in the workbook carry cached results only for the results mode;
  Excel and LibreOffice recalculate on open.
- **Leak-test note:** the ratings and credentials fixtures carry a supervisor name, email, and phone
  (seeded in `seeded_identifiers.json`) to prove ignored contact columns do not come back; the
  generator `crates/phn-server/tests/fixtures/phase18_workforce/generate.py` writes the CSVs and the
  expected values.
